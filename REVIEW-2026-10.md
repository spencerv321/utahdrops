Evidence tags work as in REVIEW.md: **[V]** verified (measured, reproduced, or read from production reports), **[C]** code-traced, **[I]** inferred.

**What I ran**
- **Live site:** fetched every public route plus the sitemap, robots.txt and `/api/health` from utahdrops.com with curl. Read titles, canonicals, JSON-LD and response headers.
- **Local copy:** Postgres 16 with the **real** DABS catalog (26,239 products, 5,463 in stock), xlsx, allocated list, drawings, one 100-SKU store pass, and the 4,543-row licensee snapshot. Ran a production build (`pnpm build && pnpm start`).
- **Screenshots and accessibility:** Playwright at 1366px and 390px @2x for 21 routes signed out, plus watchlist, home and admin signed in with the local-only auth patch (reverted). axe-core (WCAG 2.1 AA) on every route. Results are in `review/2026-10-13/`, with per-route layout and axe data in `review/2026-10-13/axe-and-layout.json`.
- **Lighthouse:** mobile profile on 6 pages, run locally.
- **Production reports:** dispatched read-only `report.yml` runs (`funnel`, `freshness`, `checks`, `discover`, `auth`, `searchlog`, `activity`): Actions runs #71–#77.
- **Dependencies:** `pnpm audit --prod`.

**Limits.** The local database has one day of history, so feed sections ("Just happened", What's new, Worth a look) are empty in the local screenshots. I judged those pages from the live HTML instead. Chromium can't load outside sites from this sandbox, so all screenshots are of the local build.

## 1. Verdict

Utah Drops is now a solid, trustworthy product. Data runs on time (34 of 34 store runs succeeded this week, all 77 watched bottles are checked on target), public pages pass WCAG AA in axe, and Google already sends half the traffic: 180 of 405 sessions, with 138 sessions landing directly on product pages. Nearly all of REVIEW.md's Phase 0–1 work has shipped.

The weak spot is no longer the engine. It's the **funnel around it**:
- Phones can't reach half the site: Worth a look, This week, What's new, Utah 101, makers and bars aren't in the mobile nav.
- **10 of 27 accounts never confirmed their sign-up email**, including two from this week.
- Only a handful of people watch bottles, so **0 alert emails went out in the last 48h** despite 546 alertable events.
- SEO stops at product pages. There are no store, category or town landing pages, and every page is rendered per request with `no-store`.

The next month should go to getting visitors from Google to a confirmed watch. More data features can wait.

## 4. Admin dashboard proposal

**Today** (`app/admin/page.tsx`, `lib/admin-metrics.ts`): live visitors, KPIs with deltas, trend chart, channels/sources/referrers/campaigns, top pages/bottles/searches/landing pages, cities/devices/browsers/countries/time of day, accounts and alerts, ratings beta, Worth a look, and job freshness. It's read-only, mostly traffic, and doesn't fit a phone screen (U8). Everything below currently needs a `report.yml` dispatch and log reading.

**Proposal:** keep the current page as an **Audience** tab and add **Operations** and **Funnel** tabs, plus a few safe **actions**.

### Tab: Funnel (from `page_events`, `discover_events`, `watch_intents`, `auth.users`)
1. **Visitor → search → product → watch tap → confirmed watch**, by day and by entry surface (search, product, discover, home). Same SQL as `report.yml` → `funnel`.
2. **Sign-up health:** accounts created, confirmed, first sign-in; a list of unconfirmed accounts > 1h old (masked email); pending and expired `watch_intents`. Source: `auth.users`, `watch_intents`.
3. **Search misses:** top zero-result queries this week, with a "copy alias candidate" helper. Source: `discover_events` (`search:*` `shown` with `results = 0`) joined to query text. This feeds the alias list in `search_key` (still added by PR, never automatically).
4. **Landing pages to sign-up:** which landing pages convert. Source: `page_events` first view per session plus `auth.users.created_at`.

### Tab: Operations (from `scrape_runs`, `store_checks`, `products`, `alert_deliveries`, `dabs_pacer`)
1. **Jobs:** the existing list, plus the last run's duration, attempted/scraped/failed counts, and a link to the Actions run. Source: `scrape_runs`.
2. **Store coverage:** % in stock checked < 6/24/72h by segment, watched on target, failing SKUs in backoff. Source: the `freshness` report queries.
3. **Alerts:** events by type vs delivered, emails sent per day, last digest time, permanent failures. Source: `inventory_events`, `alert_deliveries`. Once Resend webhooks are stored: bounces and complaints.
4. **DABS load:** requests per hour from `dabs_pacer` and `store_checks`, Check DABS now use vs `CHECK_NOW` caps, circuit-breaker state.
5. **Data-quality flags:** catalog passes rejected by the zero-qty guard, allocated rows with no matched product, products surfaced without a photo (`photosurfaced`), maker vendor-match gaps (`makers`), licensee pull age and row count vs `MIN_LICENSEES`.
6. **Content clock:** Utah 101 `checked` dates and the next May re-check, maker `checked` dates, drawing-odds snapshot date. Source: `lib/utah-101.ts`, `data/makers.json`.
